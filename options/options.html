Getting "Access Blocked / Error 403: access_denied"?

While UmSpec is in development, Google requires your Gmail account to be listed under Test Users in Google Cloud Console:

  1. Go to Google Cloud Console > OAuth consent screen.
  2. Under Test users, click + ADD USERS and enter your email address.
  3. Click Save, then click Connect Google Drive above again!

Zero-Touch Privacy: UmSpec uses the restricted drive.file scope. It can only see and modify folders and files created by UmSpec. It cannot access any personal files on your Google Drive.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How does ZIP export work?
    ZIP export runs 100% locally inside your browser. It packages lecture notes, slides, and tutorials into an organized archive folder without needing any Google account or external servers.
  • How do I resolve "Access Blocked / Error 403: access_denied"?
    In your Google Cloud Console, navigate to APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen > Test users, click + Add Users, enter your Gmail address, and save.
  • Are my SPeCTRUM passwords or credentials saved?
    No. UmSpec uses your already signed-in browser tab session. Your UM passwords and session cookies are never extracted, logged, or sent to any remote server.
  • Where do my files go in Google Drive?
    Files are placed inside a private folder named after your semester (e.g. 2026/2027 Semester 1), organized neatly by course code and topic.
