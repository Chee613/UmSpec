How Campus Caching Works

• Analyze Once, Serve 30,000: When the first student in UM opens a course outline, Gemini extracts the deadlines and caches them in Cloudflare KV for 90 days.
• Instant 5ms Hits: Subsequent students in that same class load the deadlines instantly with zero wait and 0 API calls.
• Privacy Guaranteed: No student names, matric numbers, or credentials ever touch the AI proxy.

Help, Setup & Privacy

Official documentation, privacy policies, and security architecture of UmSpec.

Privacy & Security Principles

  • Zero Credential Logging: UmSpec uses your already signed-in browser session. SPeCTRUM session cookies and Google passwords are never stored, sent to servers, or extracted.
  • Scattered Privacy Architecture: ZIP exports run 100% locally on your machine without contacting any external servers.
  • Minimal Scopes: Google Drive permissions are restricted strictly to files created by UmSpec (drive.file).
